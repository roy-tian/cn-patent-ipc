# Data sources and notes

[中文](NOTICE.md)

## Contents

| File                 | Content                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `data/ipc.jsonl`     | Chinese-edition IPC entries (部 sections, 大类 classes, 小类 subclasses, 主组 main groups, 分组 subgroups) with Chinese titles and effective editions |
| `data/sei.jsonl`     | Strategic emerging industries (SEI) rules; each industry code has one or more keyword rules                                                           |
| `data/ipc-sei.jsonl` | IPC ↔ SEI mapping                                                                                                                                     |

`version` is the IPC edition in which an entry took effect or was last amended,
from 1985.01 to 2026.01. `DATA_VERSION` exports the newest one.

## Sources

- IPC Chinese titles: CNIPA's 《国际专利分类表》, the Chinese edition of the
  IPC. WIPO publishes the IPC scheme under CC BY 4.0.
- SEI rules and mapping: CNIPA's
  《战略性新兴产业分类与国际专利分类参照关系表》.

The data began as a downstream project's seed dump of these publications and
has been corrected twice:

- v0.1.2 rebuilt the Chinese titles from the CNIPA 2026.01 PDFs (all 8
  sections). About 12% of the dump's titles were misaligned: carried over from
  a neighbouring entry, merged with notes or subclass indexes, or truncated.
  `level` and `version` still come from the dump.
- v0.1.3 rebuilt the `E01D101/00` indexing group from page 11 of the E-section
  PDF. The PDF wraps these codes across two lines, and the dump had cut off
  their last digit, merging 11 entries into 5.

## Known quirks (kept as published)

- 2 entries have empty titles, `C12P19/64` and `H02K21/24`, because the
  official PDF prints none.
- There is no `H01L` subclass. IPC moved its content into the new `H10` class
  over three editions: H10B, H10K and H10N in 2023.01, H10D, H10F and H10H in
  2025.01, and H10P and H10W in 2026.01, which no longer has H01L. This was
  confirmed against the CNIPA 2026.01 H section and WIPO's IPC 2026.01 master
  files. The official `G09G` title still cross-references H01L and is kept as
  published.
- 257 entries have a `NULL` `version`, and 1,688 subgroups have a `NULL`
  `level` because the dump has none. Sections, classes and subclasses have a
  `NULL` `level` by design.
- The mapping matches exact codes only. A subclass mapping doesn't cover the
  groups under it.
- Titles come from PDF text, so a few may keep layout artifacts, such as an
  unclosed parenthesis where the PDF breaks a line, or a stray edition marker.
  `C07C409/02` and its siblings really do begin with `-O-O-` (a peroxide bond).

## Not included

8 withdrawn subgroups that pre-2020 filings used and that existed only in a
downstream application's database. Add them yourself if you need them.

## Maintenance

- `data/*.jsonl` is the source of truth. Apply corrections and new editions to
  it directly and check them against the official CNIPA publication. The
  one-off seed importer was removed because re-running it would undo the
  v0.1.2 title rebuild, and the PDF aligner used for that rebuild isn't in the
  repository.
- `sql/postgresql/*.sql` is generated by `npm run generate:sql`. Don't edit it
  by hand.
